# Gaps found while building the mobile app

This is a running list of problems found during the mobile app work. They are **parked until the app is finished** (decided 9 Nov 2026). Each entry says where the problem is, what it affects, and the proposed fix. When one is fixed, move it to "Done" with its PR.

The plan these phases follow is [mobile-app-plan.md](mobile-app-plan.md).

## Website and backend

| # | Gap | Where | Impact | Proposed fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | **Nomination photos can't be uploaded.** No migration creates storage policies for the `candidate-photos` bucket, so uploads fail with "violates row-level security policy". `nominateCandidate` uploads with the member's own client. | `utils/elections/handlers.ts`; Supabase storage | Nominating with a photo fails on the website and in the app (seen locally). Production is unverified; a policy may have been added by hand in the dashboard. | Check production Storage › Policies. If missing, add a migration that lets an authenticated member upload under a path they own. That means changing the upload path from `<uid>_<ts>.<ext>` to `<uid>/<ts>.<ext>`. *Access-rule change: needs approval.* |
| W2 | **Draft elections are visible** to every signed-in user. The website's list applies no status or `is_active` filter, and RLS allows the read. | W3 | **Any active member can download another member's contribution receipt** by changing the ID in the URL. | W5 | **Placeholder pages** that the app's Resources tab links to: `/resources`, `/youth-programs`, `/get-help` ("Under construction" or a bare heading). `/resources/immigration` requires a website sign-in, so the app leaves it out. | `app/(site)/*` | Thin content; App Review may notice. | Write the content, or drop the rows from the app until it exists. |

| W7 | **Confusing column name:** `members.stripe_customer_id` holds `customers.id` (the user's UUID), not the Stripe ID. The website's nominate form lists every `election_positions` row, while the app lists only the positions for the election's type. | `app/(portal)/elections/[id]`, nominate form | Small inconsistencies; no wrong votes are possible, because the server checks. | Align the website with the vote handlers' rule and the election type's positions. |
| W9 | **Payments with no matching member** show a blank name on the website's Funds page. The app shows "Not in the directory". | `app/(portal)/community-funds` | Cosmetic. | Use the same wording on the website. |
| W10 | **The website's donate form can charge a monthly gift once.** It picks the price with `find(interval === frequency \|\| type === 'one_time')`, which returns whichever price comes first. The app's route had the same line and is fixed (`2578bb8`). | `components/forms/donate-form.tsx:156` | A donor who chose monthly or yearly may get a one-time charge, depending on the order Supabase returns the prices. Never an overcharge. | Use the same rule as the app: the one-time price for one-time gifts, otherwise the recurring price with the matching interval. **High priority.** |
| W11 | **Abandoned payment attempts leave pending donation rows.** Each card or bank attempt saves a pending `donations` row before Stripe opens; leaving Stripe or trying again leaves it pending. | `submitDonation` (website and app) | Pending rows that never complete clutter the records. | Expire pending card/bank rows when the Checkout session expires (webhook `checkout.session.expired`), or create the row only on success. |
| W12 | **Donations are matched to donors by email.** `getOrCreateDonor` looks donors up by email, so a gift entered with another person's email is filed under their donor record. | `utils/donation/handlers.ts` | Misattributed gifts, and the giver can't see their own gift (RLS). | For signed-in givers, match on `user_id` first. |
| W13 | **The app's receipt routes report problems as redirects to website pages**, not `{ error }` JSON, because they wrap the website's PDF routes. | W15 | **Nominations are refused at the exact start and end moments** on the website's election page (`isBefore`/`isAfter`), while the server accepts them. | `app/(portal)/elections/[id]/page.tsx:235-239` | Edge case: the page hides Nominate for the first and last instant. | Match the server's inclusive window (`utils/elections/handlers.ts:719-722`). |
| W16 | **The "Candidate voting" timeline step shows whenever `candidate_voting_end` is set**, even when separate voting periods are off and that date is ignored. | `app/(portal)/elections/[id]/page.tsx:333` | A misleading step on the timeline. | Show it only when separate periods are on (the app already does). |
| W17 | **Results wording after voting closes:** the website still says "Leading" and "Currently Passing/Failing/Tied", and a closed initiative with no votes says "Tied". | `components/elections/results-components.tsx:323-330, 423-436` | Final results read as provisional. | "Elected" from `candidate-results`, and "Supported / Opposed / Tied / No votes", as the app does. |
| W18 | **The candidate page's status** shows the raw `elections.status`, or "Candidate Voting Closed" based on `end_date`, ignoring the candidate window. | `app/(portal)/elections/[id]/candidate/[candidateId]/page.tsx:108-116, 289-292` | Wrong status during separate voting periods. | Use the shared election phase. |
| W19 | **The website promises receipt emails that aren't sent:** "We email a receipt" and "we email a tax receipt for every gift", while its own complete page says no email is sent. | `components/forms/donate-form.tsx:341, 497, 546`; `app/(site)/donate/confirmation/[donationId]/page.tsx:95, 140` | Donors wait for an email that never comes. | Remove the promise, or start sending receipts. |
| W20 | **A refunded donation shows "We're confirming your payment" for ever** and keeps refreshing: any status other than completed or failed takes the pending branch. | `app/(site)/donate/confirmation/[donationId]/page.tsx:79-116` | Wrong state, endless polling. | Add a refunded state, as the app has. |
| W21 | **The donate form's options and the schema disagree.** It offers every active program, though `DonationFormSchema` accepts only `DONATION_PURPOSES`. It also offers Monthly and Yearly even when no recurring price is active, and "Other amount" pre-fills 1000.00. Its pending copy always says "Bank transfers can take a few days", even for cards. | `components/forms/donate-form.tsx:324` and the confirmation page | A purpose the schema rejects; a frequency that can't be charged. | Offer only what can be accepted, as the app does. |
| W22 | **Closed fundraisers still show Donate** on the card and "Donate Now" on the detail page (the donate page then refuses). The detail page says "Ends 2 days ago". An unnamed donor shows a blank name. | `components/fundraisers/fundraiser-card.tsx`, `fundraiser-details.tsx:82-88` | Confusing dead ends. | Hide Donate when closed and say "Ended {date}", as the app does. |
| W23 | **Funds "Recent donations" includes pending and failed gifts**, though its subtitle says "Completed gifts" (`getDonations` doesn't filter by status). Expenses are sorted by `created_at`, not `date`. | `app/(portal)/community-funds/page.tsx:78, 148-150` | Totals and the list overstate donations. | Filter to completed; sort by `date`. |
| W24 | **Plan amounts ignore the family quantity.** Checkout sets the subscription quantity to the family size, capped at 1–5. The website's plan card shows the unit price only (understated), and its directory multiplies by `totalMembersInFamily` with no cap (overstated for families over 5). | `components/ui/AccountForms/CustomerPortalForm.tsx:40-42, 65`; `components/member-list.tsx:64-66, 87` | Wrong amounts shown to members. | Unit price × subscription quantity (capped 1–5). |
| W25 | **Member status wording and rules differ:** the website badge uses `status` only ("Active", "Pending", "Rejected"), while the app also requires `isApproved` for active. The directory says "Yet to choose contribution plan" in red. `/register` shows the form again to people who already applied. | `components/portal/member-row.tsx:9-21`, `components/member-list.tsx`, `app/(site)/register` | Inconsistent states across website and app. | One shared member-state rule, and show the application's state on `/register`. |
| W26 | **"Elected unopposed" shows before voting has started.** `shouldShowUnopposedStatus` only checks that candidate voting isn't open, so during nominations the website shows the "Candidates elected unopposed" banner and "Unopposed" badges for any single-candidate position. | `utils/election-config.ts` (`shouldShowUnopposedStatus`), `app/(portal)/elections/[id]` | Candidates are announced as elected while nominations are still open. | Require candidate voting to have closed, as the app does (`utils/elections/rules.ts`, `electedUnopposed`). |
| W27 | **Anonymous fundraiser gifts are readable by anyone.** `fundraiser_donations` has a "Donations are viewable by everyone" policy (`SELECT … true`) beside "View non-anonymous donations". Policies are OR-ed, so the narrower one has no effect: any visitor can read every gift, including the donor name and message of anonymous gifts, by querying the table. | Supabase RLS on `fundraiser_donations` | **Privacy leak** of anonymous donors (found while testing the app's gift result screen). | Drop the `true` policy; keep public reads to non-anonymous gifts (plus the donor's own); serve anonymous gifts' totals through a view or the server. *RLS change: needs approval.* **High priority.** |
